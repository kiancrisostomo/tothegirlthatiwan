to my piz, the person i never expected to fall for.

hi, piz.

i don't really know where to start, honestly. i've been wanting to put these feelings into words, but every time i try, parang ang daming gustong lumabas sa isip ko at hindi ko alam kung alin ang uunahin ko. there's just so much i want to say to you, and somehow, words still feel so small compared to what i actually feel.

pero sige, susubukan ko.

piz, i didn't expect this. not even a little bit.

i didn't expect na darating ako sa puntong hahanapin-hanapin kita, na mamimiss kita kahit hindi naman tayo, na magiging masaya ako just because i get to see you, or that the simplest things would remind me of you. i didn't expect na darating yung araw na makakakita lang ako ng hello kitty or anything sanrio-related, and suddenly, ikaw na agad ang unang papasok sa isip ko. it's funny, actually. dati, mga cute things lang naman sila, but now, somehow, they have a little piece of you in them.

and i think that's one of the things about liking someone. you don't really notice when it starts. one day, they're just someone you talk to on the internet, someone you laugh with, someone you find funny. then, before you even realize it, they've become someone you look forward to, someone you worry about, someone whose happiness matters to you more than you ever planned.

and then suddenly, you're here, writing a whole letter about them, wondering how the hell you got this attached.

HAHAHAHA. PIZ, ANO BA.

but seriously, i think one of the first things that drew me to you was how funny you are. you're genuinely funny, and i don't think you understand how much i appreciate that about you. you're the kind of person who can make an ordinary conversation feel special without even trying. there's something so comforting about your humor, about the way you talk, about the way you can make me laugh without having to do anything extraordinary.

it's like i found someone who somehow mirrors a part of me. someone who gets my kind of humor, someone i can be weird around, someone i don't have to impress all the time. i don't have to pretend to be someone more interesting, more likable, or more deserving of attention.

i can just be me.

and that's rare, piz. really rare.

because there are so many people in this world, but not everyone makes you feel like you can put your guard down. not everyone makes you feel like you can exist without constantly worrying about how you're being perceived. not everyone makes you feel safe enough to show the parts of yourself that you usually keep hidden.

but somehow, you do.

you became this little safe space for me without even knowing it. and i don't know if that's something you've always been capable of doing or if it's just something that happens when you're around the right person, but i'm grateful that i got to experience it with you.

and the more i got to know you, the more i realized that it wasn't just your humor that made me admire you.

it's your kindness. the way you're thoughtful. the way you seem to have this softness in you that makes me want to be careful with your heart. there's something about you that makes me think,

this is someone i wouldn't want to hurt.

you know that feeling when you meet someone so precious to you that even the thought of being the reason they cry makes your heart ache? that's how i see you, piz. parang ang hirap mong saktan. not because you're weak, but because there's something about you that makes me want to be gentle with you. as if the world has already been unkind enough sometimes, and if i ever get the chance to be part of your life, i would want to be one of the people who make it a little easier for you.

and i think that's what makes you different to me.

you're not just someone who's funny and easy to talk to. you're someone whose character i genuinely admire. someone who doesn't just go along with everything for the sake of being accepted. someone who, in my eyes, has something real about them.

i didn't expect to be this drawn to you. i thought it would just be a simple crush, something light, something i'd eventually laugh about. but somewhere along the way, my feelings became a little more serious than i intended them to be.

and the thing is, i don't even know exactly when it happened.

maybe it was in the middle of one of our conversations. maybe it was when you made me laugh at something so stupid that i couldn't stop smiling afterward. maybe it was in the little things you did without realizing how much they meant to me.

or maybe it was the moment i saw you hurting.

i don't know if you'll understand what i mean, but there's something that happened to me when i saw you sad for the first time. when i saw you crying, when i saw you breaking down, when i saw you in a moment where you weren't the funny, cheerful person i was used to seeing.

it hurt.

and that surprised me, because i remember thinking,

why does this hurt me this much?

we're friends. we talk. we have our little moments, and yes, there's a little landian sometimes, HAHAHA. but who was i to feel this kind of ache just because you were hurting? who was i to feel like i wanted to take away even a little bit of the heaviness you were carrying?

i couldn't explain it then, and honestly, i still don't know how to explain it perfectly now.

all i knew was that i didn't like seeing you like that. i didn't like knowing that someone as kind and precious as you could feel so much pain. i wished i could make things easier for you, even just for a moment. i wished i could tell you that you didn't deserve whatever was hurting you, that you didn't have to be okay all the time, that you were allowed to fall apart, too.

and maybe that's when something changed in me.

because before that, i could tell myself that you were just an internet friend. someone i happened to meet, someone i happened to enjoy talking to.

but why did it ache when you were hurting?

why did your sadness feel heavier than it should have?

why did i suddenly care about things i couldn't even control?

i guess that's the thing about feelings. sometimes, they grow quietly, in places you don't even notice, until one day you realize that someone has become important to you in ways you never planned.

and, piz, you became important to me.

more than i expected. more than i was prepared for.

i wish i could tell you all of this without feeling embarrassed. i wish i could just say what i feel without that little fear in the back of my mind reminding me that i might be getting ahead of myself, that maybe i'm feeling too much, that maybe i'm the only one who sees things this way.

because, honestly,

i'm scared.

i'm scared of being too obvious. scared of saying too much. scared that one day, you'll read all of this and realize just how much space you've taken up in my heart.

and i'm scared of rejection, too.

i know i've already told you that i like you, but i don't think that means the fear suddenly disappears. if anything, admitting it made me more aware of how vulnerable i am. because before i told you, i could keep everything to myself. i could admire you quietly, pretend that my feelings weren't as deep as they were, and convince myself that i was perfectly fine with simply liking you from a distance.

but once you know, there's no taking back the fact that i let you see a little piece of my heart.

and now, i don't know what to do with all these feelings except be honest about them.

i like you, piz.

i like you in the simplest and most complicated way possible. i like the way you make me laugh. i like how thoughtful you are. i like the way you make conversations feel less lonely. i like that i can be myself around you. i like the little things about you that you probably don't even notice. i like the person you are when you're happy, and i care about the person you are when you're not.

i like you even in the moments when i don't know what to say to you. even when i miss you and have no particular reason to tell you that i do. even when something reminds me of you and all i can do is smile to myself like an idiot.

and maybe this is embarrassing to admit, but sometimes, i wish i could tell you about every little thing that reminds me of you.

like when i see hello kitty and think,

piz would probably make me think of her even more if she knew this.

or when something funny happens and you're the kind of person i'd want to tell because i already know that you'd understand why it's funny.

or when i'm having one of those days when i don't particularly feel like being anyone, and i remember that talking to you makes being myself feel a little easier.

it's not always about grand gestures or romantic moments. sometimes, it's just the little things. the ordinary moments that become special because, somehow, you're part of them.

and i think that's how i know that what i feel isn't just about the idea of having you.

it's about you. the actual you.

the person i've come to know, the person who makes me laugh, the person whose kindness i admire, the person i worry about, and the person i hope gets to experience the kind of happiness she deserves.

i don't know if you'll ever see yourself the way i see you, piz. but if there's one thing i wish you could understand, it's that you don't have to do anything extraordinary to be someone worth caring about.

you don't have to make everyone laugh. you don't have to be strong all the time. you don't have to hide your sadness just because people are used to seeing you smile.

you are still precious when you're quiet. you're still worth knowing when you're not at your best. you're still deserving of kindness on the days when you don't know how to give that kindness to yourself.

someone who admires your heart.

someone who thinks you're a little more special than you probably realize.

someone who is genuinely happy that, out of all the people in this world, she got to meet you.

i just want you to know.

i want you to know that somewhere along the way, you became someone i genuinely admire. that you made me feel something i didn't expect to feel. that you reminded me how beautiful it can be to meet someone who makes you feel safe enough to be yourself.

and if all i ever get to be is someone who admires you from afar, then maybe, someday, i'll learn to be okay with that, too.

because liking you has never been just about wanting you to be mine. at least, not entirely. it's also about being happy that you exist, about wanting good things to find you, about hoping that life becomes gentler with you, and about wishing you the kind of happiness that stays even after the laughter fades.
